# Privacy Policy for Mileaged

**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Mileaged ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

## 2. Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.**

The App is designed to operate entirely on your device. All data created, entered, and processed within the App remains on your device only.

## 3. What Data is Stored Locally

When you use Mileaged, the following information is stored **only on your device** using local storage mechanisms:

- Vehicle information (names, images)
- Mileage history and records
- Refueling history and costs
- Calculated fuel efficiency metrics
- App preferences (dark mode, unit preferences, accent color)
- Google Drive backup settings (optional user choice)

This data is never uploaded to any server unless you explicitly choose to sync with Google Drive for backup purposes.

## 4. Google Drive Integration

The App offers an optional feature to back up your data to Google Drive for synchronization across devices. If you choose to use this feature:

- You must manually authorize access to your Google Drive
- Only the backup data you explicitly choose to upload is sent to Google Drive
- You have complete control over this data and can delete backups at any time
- We do not have access to your Google Drive account beyond the backups you create
- Google's own Privacy Policy applies to data stored in Google Drive

## 5. Permissions

The App requests the following device permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Read External Storage | To access gallery images when you choose to add vehicle photos |
| Read Media Images (Android 13+) | To access image picker on modern Android versions |
| Internet | To sync data with Google Drive (optional feature) |

**We only access these permissions when you explicitly trigger an action** (e.g., selecting a photo, syncing with Google Drive).

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

### Google Sign-In
- Used only for Google Drive backup functionality
- You must explicitly authorize this
- Authentication is handled by Google's secure infrastructure
- We do not store your Google credentials

### Google Drive
- Optional backup storage service
- Governed by Google's Privacy Policy
- You have full control over what data is synced

## 7. Data You Control

**You have complete control over all your data:**

- **Export Data:** Export all vehicle records and mileage data in JSON format
- **Import Data:** Restore data from previous backups
- **Clear All Data:** Delete all data from the App with a single action
- **No Automatic Upload:** Data is never automatically sent anywhere

## 8. Security

Your data's security depends on your device's security:

- Data is stored in the App's private sandbox on your device
- Only the App and system apps can access this data
- If your device is compromised, your data could be at risk (same as any local app)
- We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated

## 9. What We Don't Do

✗ We do not collect usage analytics
✗ We do not track your location
✗ We do not use advertisements
✗ We do not sell your data
✗ We do not send telemetry
✗ We do not build user profiles
✗ We do not access your contacts, messages, or other personal data
✗ We do not require account creation or sign-in

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted in the App's release notes and this document.

## 12. Compliance

This App complies with:

- **Google Play's Privacy Requirements**
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation)
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- **Other regional privacy laws**

Since we don't collect personal data, most privacy regulations don't apply, but we respect all privacy laws regardless.

## 13. Children's Privacy

Mileaged is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If a child has provided information through the App, please contact us to have it removed.

## 14. Summary

**Mileaged is designed with privacy as the default:**

- All your data stays on your device
- No tracking, no analytics, no data collection
- You own your data completely
- Optional backups are only created with your consent
- No third-party access to your vehicle or mileage information

**Your privacy is our priority.**
