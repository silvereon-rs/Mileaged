Welcome to Mileaged - The Simple, Private Vehicle Mileage Tracker

Track your vehicle's fuel efficiency, mileage, and refueling history with complete privacy. All your data stays on your device. No tracking, no ads, no data collection.

🚗 CORE FEATURES

✓ Vehicle Management
  - Add unlimited vehicles
  - Store custom images for each vehicle
  - Switch between vehicles with one tap
  - Edit or delete vehicles anytime

✓ Mileage Tracking (Tank-to-Tank Method)
  - Record fuel filled at each pump
  - Automatic mileage calculation
  - Historical data tracking
  - Mean fuel efficiency calculation

✓ Refueling History
  - Track fuel costs
  - Monitor fuel price trends
  - View detailed refueling records
  - Calculate total spending per vehicle

✓ Data Management
  - Export all data as JSON backup
  - Import previous backups
  - Clear all data with one action
  - No complicated setup required

✓ Customization
  - Multiple accent colors
  - Dark mode support
  - Choose metric (km/l) or imperial (mpg) units
  - Persistent preferences

✓ Backup & Sync (Optional)
  - Manually sync to Google Drive
  - Restore from Google Drive backups
  - Full control over backup data
  - No automatic uploads

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST

Your privacy is our priority:

✗ We do NOT collect any personal data
✗ We do NOT use analytics or tracking
✗ We do NOT display advertisements
✗ We do NOT sell your data
✗ We do NOT access your contacts or location
✗ We do NOT require account sign-in

All vehicle records, mileage history, and refueling data remains entirely on your device in a secure local database. You have complete control over your data at all times.

• Read External Storage - Access gallery images when you add vehicle photos

Data Storage:

💡 HOW IT WORKS

1. Add Your Vehicle - Enter vehicle name and optional photo
2. Record Refueling - Input fuel amount and optional cost each time you refuel
3. Track Mileage - When you fill up again, the app calculates efficiency automatically
4. Monitor Trends - View detailed history and statistics for each vehicle
5. Export or Backup - Download your data or sync to Google Drive anytime
